# Paradigm Assist: questions you can ask

Generated from the chatbot's rules (`src/api/assist/fastpath`): 162 example questions in 24 groups.

These are answered **instantly, without AI**, straight from the Paradigm database. Every answer respects your role: you only see your own data, your team's, or the whole organisation's, depending on your access.

**Tips**
- Use names as they appear in the app ("kavya", "isaac roy"). If several people match, the bot asks which one.
- Add a period: today, yesterday, this week, last month, "in june", "on 25 nov", "last 7 days".
- Simple Hinglish works too: "kavya kitne din kaam kiya", "isaac kahan hai aaj", "meri chutti balance".
- Anything else (SOPs, how-to procedures, drafting, general questions) is still answered by the AI assistant.
